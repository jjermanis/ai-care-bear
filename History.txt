bring it down. 
I'm curious how collision will work. Will it be the entire rectangle, or just where the
pixels are? We shall see.

version 01.04	Me 17 min	Gemini 5 min	Test 2 min	Total 1 hour 34

One of the issues that was noticed was that the transparency in the Care Bear PNG
image did not work. The grid on the image looked like a marker for transparency.
But it was an actual grid. I asked Gemini to replace that image with transparency.
It failed three times - changes, but no transparency. As a sanity check, I went to
ChatGPT to implement transparency. It worked on the first attempt. To work around
this issue, I asked Gemini to make the background the same color as used in the 
game. That did work. Less easy to change the ground color... but fine for now.


version 01.05	Me 7 min	Gemini 2 min	Test 4 min	Total 1 hour 47

This was one of the original rules of the game:
"No treasure nor traps touch anything else on the screen."
This was not followed at all. Gemini implemented this to not put pieces on top of
each other. It added a 1-pixel zone around each pixel to avoid added pieces. This
was quick and worked well.
To add some convenience, I wanted the Play Again button to also supply clicking 
the Enter key with that button shown. Gemini provided a quick easy change for
that.
Finally, I added a little change to the random locations of items. I wanted to move 
them off the right edge, and a few rows from the bottom so they wouldn't hit players
at the beginning of the game before moving. This was easy to change manually.
